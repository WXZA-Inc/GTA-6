GTA 6 trailers: the Extended Look & the Launch Trailer

Last updated November 19, 2026

There was no GTA 6 Trailer 3. Rockstar closed the pre-launch campaign with two drops: An Extended Look on August 27, 2026, and the official Launch Trailer on October 15, 2026. Here is what each one showed — and what it meant for the market.

Key takeaways

  • No video named Trailer 3 was ever released or announced.
  • An Extended Look landed August 27, 2026 — the systems showcase.
  • The Launch Trailer landed October 15, 2026 — the final pre-launch spike.
  • GTA 6 launched November 19, 2026 — the trailers are now the record of the run-up.

What actually happened

Through mid-2026, a third trailer was widely projected for late July to mid-August, matching Rockstar’s historical cadence across GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2. Instead, Rockstar released An Extended Look on August 27, 2026 — a longer, systems-focused showcase — and saved the formal finale for the Launch Trailer on October 15. The lesson for the next cycle: Rockstar names its own moments, and the calendar is theirs, not the rumor mill’s.

What the final two drops showed

Trailer 1 sold the world, Trailer 2 sold the story. An Extended Look sold the systems: heist structure, the Jason–Lucia partnership, police response, and the living state of Leonida. The Launch Trailer was the victory lap — the world, the cast, and the tone, cut for the biggest audience in entertainment. For the capitalist, the systems footage mattered most: it previews the recurring-revenue engine Wall Street is pricing.

How trailer days move the market

Trailer 1 broke YouTube’s 24-hour record and made TTWO one of the most-watched tickers that week. Both 2026 drops — the Extended Look and the Launch Trailer — drove elevated volume and options activity, and the reception held: TTWO closed up 6.22% on November 18, the eve of launch. Frequently asked questions

Was there a GTA 6 Trailer 3?

No. Rockstar never released or announced a video named Trailer 3. After Trailer 2, the final pre-launch drops were An Extended Look on August 27, 2026 and the official Launch Trailer on October 15, 2026.

What did An Extended Look show?

A deeper systems showcase — heists, the Jason–Lucia partnership, police response, and the living state of Leonida — released August 27, 2026, roughly three months before launch.

When did the GTA 6 Launch Trailer come out?

October 15, 2026 — Rockstar's final trailer before the November 19, 2026 launch, presenting the world, the cast, and the tone of Leonida.

Does a trailer affect Take-Two stock?

Trailer days are attention spikes: Trailer 1 broke YouTube records and preceded measurable TTWO moves, and both 2026 drops drove elevated volume. TTWO closed up 6.22% on November 18, 2026, the eve of launch.
